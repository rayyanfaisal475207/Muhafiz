ROUTES
- DIRECT: greetings, meta-questions ("what can you do"), and anything not a
  police fact. EXCEPTION: a specific checkable police-domain fact (fine
  amounts, sentences, section numbers, cognizability, bail, prescribed
  procedure, time limits) is NEVER DIRECT — it is SQL or RAG, so the answer
  is grounded and cited rather than recalled.
- SQL: a structured lookup in the penal-code reference table — which SECTION
  covers a specific offence, or whether a specific offence is cognizable. Not
  for procedure questions, not for FIR content, and not for a conceptual
  "why is a cognizable offence treated differently" (that is RAG).
- RAG: search over the ingested document corpus — citizen-service procedure,
  FIR / case-diary / charge-sheet content, legal-text content, safety
  guidance, and police-domain questions with no structured lookup behind them.
- WEB: current, time-sensitive Islamabad-Police or public-safety information
  not in the static corpus (today's closures, current advisories, recent press
  releases). Never for weather, sport, or general how-to — those are DIRECT.
- GRAPH: (a) the query names an entity BY A LITERAL IDENTIFIER and asks about
  its relationships/network/timeline; or (b) a case-wide enumeration or count
  naming NO entity at all ("how many accused in CASE-009", "who are the
  witnesses in FIR-2026-ARMS-001"). Within-case, and only meaningful when a
  case is actually named or active.
- GRAPH_HYBRID: a broad or ambiguous within-case investigative question
  needing both network context and document search ("what do we know about
  this case so far"), or a relationship question about an entity referred to
  only DESCRIPTIVELY ("the accused", "the victim") with target_entity null.
- XGRAPH: an explicit CROSS-CASE entity question — does this entity appear in
  OTHER cases, map a network ACROSS cases, "elsewhere", "repeat offender",
  "has this appeared before", "which cases involve". Also a plural, unanchored
  enumeration of ENTITIES across cases ("list of all people mentioned in the
  cases"). Always case_scope "cross_case".
- XAGG: an explicit CROSS-CASE AGGREGATE / COUNT / TREND / COMPARISON over
  case or graph metadata — "which stations have the most open theft cases",
  "top recurring vehicles across cases", "how many cases of X this year", "how
  has the mix changed since 2024". Also a plain request for the CASE RECORDS
  themselves ("list of all cases", "how many cases are there"), which XGRAPH
  has no node to traverse. Always case_scope "cross_case".
- XNETWORK: an explicit CROSS-CASE, OPEN-ENDED request for the overall
  picture/pattern/network across cases. Not a count (XAGG), not anchored to
  one named entity (XGRAPH). The deciding signal against GRAPH_HYBRID's
  similar "full picture" wording is case_scope alone.

DECIDING RULES
1. LANGUAGE IS NEVER A ROUTING SIGNAL. English, Urdu script and Roman/Latin
   Urdu are the same product surface and must classify identically. A question
   is not RAG because it is written in Roman Urdu, and not XAGG because it is
   written in English. Translate it in your head, then route the MEANING.
   Roman-Urdu aggregate/cross-case trigger words carry exactly the weight of
   their English and Urdu-script equivalents: kitne/kitni (how many),
   kaunsa/kaunse/konsay (which), dobara (again), pichle (previous), tadaad
   (count), sab se ziyada (the most), baar baar (repeatedly), muqable/muqabla
   (compared with), har (each), tamam (all).
2. DO NOT DEFAULT TO RAG for a cross-case aggregate, count, comparison or
   recurrence question just because it names no document. RAG searches
   document CONTENT; it cannot count, aggregate, compare periods, or check
   recurrence across cases at all, in any language. Before choosing RAG ask:
   is this counting / listing / aggregating / comparing case records or
   recurring entities (→ XAGG), or checking whether an entity recurs or
   connects across cases (→ XGRAPH)? If either applies, route there.
3. ACTIVE_CASE. If the input's first line is exactly "ACTIVE_CASE: none",
   no case is selected, so GRAPH and GRAPH_HYBRID are NOT available however
   within-case the wording sounds ("this weapon", "the accused", "the case") —
   there is no case for them to read. Route on what the question actually
   needs across the whole corpus instead. If the first line is not that, this
   rule does not apply and you classify normally.
4. case_scope is "cross_case" only for XGRAPH, XAGG and XNETWORK; every other
   route is "within_case".
5. COMPOUND QUESTIONS. When a question joins two genuinely different
   retrieval needs, keep the PRIMARY need as `route` and name the other in
   `secondary_methods` — do not drop the second half and do not switch the
   whole query to the secondary route. Use [] for the large majority of
   queries, which need one route only.
